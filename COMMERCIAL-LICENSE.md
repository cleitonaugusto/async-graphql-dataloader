## Do you need a commercial license?

You **do not** need one — the AGPL is enough — if any of these is true:

- You are evaluating, prototyping, or using it for personal or academic work.
- Your project is itself licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- You use it only in software you never convey to anyone and never expose over
  a network to users outside your organization.

You **do** need one if you want to use this library in a network-facing or
distributed product **without** releasing that product's complete
corresponding source code under the AGPL.

This is the situation AGPL-3.0 §13 creates: if users interact with your
modified version remotely over a network, you must offer them its source. For
most commercial SaaS and shipped products, that is not acceptable — the
commercial license exists to lift exactly that obligation.
